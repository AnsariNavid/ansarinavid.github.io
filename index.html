Munich, Germany

Navid Ansari

AI researcher working on efficient language models and optimization.

I’m a Senior Strategic AI Researcher at Huawei’s Heisenberg Research Center. At Amazon Web Services, I worked on structured sparsity for large language models. My doctoral research at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics focused on using neural networks to solve inverse design problems.

Research

Efficient language models

Hardware-aware inference and post-training sparsity for large language models.

Optimization and inverse design

Parallel Bayesian optimization, mixed-integer methods, and uncertainty-aware neural inversion for computational design.

Agentic AI

Retrieval and tool use for literature discovery, research monitoring, and reporting.
